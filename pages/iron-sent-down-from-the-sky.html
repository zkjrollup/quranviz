Modern "Scientific Miracle" Claims

Was iron "sent down" from the sky?

Surah 57:25
“

The verse states that God "sent down iron, in which is great strength and benefits for mankind."

Context A popular apologetic argument connects this to the real astrophysical fact that iron on Earth ultimately originates from supernova nucleosynthesis, framing it as remarkable foreknowledge. Linguists and classical commentators point out that "sent down" (anzalna) is a routine Quranic idiom applied to ordinary provisions — cattle, clothing, and rain are all described the same way — making it a stretch to read this one instance as a specific claim about the interstellar origin of a metal.
