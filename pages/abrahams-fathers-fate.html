Contradictions

Did Abraham pray for his father, or disown him?

Surah 9:114, 19:47 vs. 60:4
“

One passage has Abraham promising to ask forgiveness for his father; another has Abraham and his followers explicitly disowning their disbelieving relatives.

Context Commentators generally read 9:114 as clarifying that Abraham's promise was conditional and later withdrawn once his father's disbelief became clear — a resolution some critics find like a patch applied after the fact.
Contested Wording (Arabic & Transliteration)
Surah 9:114
فَلَمَّا تَبَيَّنَ لَهُ أَنَّهُ عَدُوٌّ لِّلَّهِ تَبَرَّأَ مِنْهُ
fa-lammā tabayyana lahu annahu 'aduwwun lillāhi tabarra'a minhu
"but when it became clear to him that he was an enemy of God, he disowned him (tabarra'a)"
Surah 19:47
سَأَسْتَغْفِرُ لَكَ رَبِّي
sa-astaghfiru laka rabbī
"I will ask forgiveness for you from my Lord"
Surah 60:4
إِنَّا بُرَآءُ مِنكُمْ
innā bura'ā'u minkum
"indeed, we are disassociated (bura'ā'u) from you"
