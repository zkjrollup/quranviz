Scientific & Historical Absurdities

What was man created from?

at least 8 distinct materials across 24 verses (see full list below)
“

Man's creation is described using at least eight distinct materials or starting points across roughly two dozen verses: water, dust, clay (in several distinct phrasings — sounding clay, sticky clay, clay like pottery, mud), a fluid or drop of seed, a clot, and simply "nothing."

Context The sheer number of distinct phrasings (see the full list below) is itself the point of contention: is this a single process described from different angles across a long ministry — dust as origin, water/fluid as the reproductive stage, clay as a poetic composite, culminating in the clot/lump stages of embryonic development — or is it evidence of independent, loosely-reconciled sayings on the same question, repeated across many contexts without ever being harmonized into one consistent account? Traditional tafsir generally reads these as sequential stages of one unified process (dust → clay → fluid → clot → lump), while critics note that several verses (e.g. "from water" alone, or "from nothing") don't explicitly fit that sequence when read on their own terms.
