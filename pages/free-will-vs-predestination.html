Contradictions

Does the Quran teach free will, or predestination?

Surah 74:31, 81:29 vs. 18:29, 76:3
“

Some verses describe God sending astray or guiding whom He wills; others tell listeners "whoever wills, let him believe" and describe humans as shown the way, "be he grateful or ungrateful."

Context This tension produced entire theological schools within Islam (Qadariyya vs. Jabriyya, and later Ash'ari vs. Mu'tazila positions) — it's a genuine, historically significant fault line in Islamic theology, not a fringe reading.
Contested Wording (Arabic & Transliteration)
Surah 74:31
يُضِلُّ اللَّهُ مَن يَشَاءُ وَيَهْدِي مَن يَشَاءُ
yuḍillu Allāhu man yashā'u wa yahdī man yashā'
"God sends astray whom He wills and guides whom He wills"
Surah 81:29
وَمَا تَشَاءُونَ إِلَّا أَن يَشَاءَ اللَّهُ
wa mā tashā'ūna illā an yashā'a Allāh
"and you do not will except that God wills"
Surah 18:29
فَمَن شَاءَ فَلْيُؤْمِن وَمَن شَاءَ فَلْيَكْفُرْ
fa-man shā'a fal-yu'min wa man shā'a fal-yakfur
"so whoever wills, let him believe; and whoever wills, let him disbelieve"
Surah 76:3
إِنَّا هَدَيْنَاهُ السَّبِيلَ إِمَّا شَاكِرًا وَإِمَّا كَفُورًا
innā hadaynāhu al-sabīla immā shākiran wa immā kafūrā
"We guided him to the way, be he grateful or ungrateful"
