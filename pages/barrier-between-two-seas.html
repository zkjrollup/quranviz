Modern "Scientific Miracle" Claims

Does the Quran describe a scientific barrier between fresh and salt water?

Surah 25:53, 55:19-20, 27:61
“

Describes God releasing two bodies of water, one fresh and one salty, with a barrier and "forbidding partition" between them that they do not cross.

Context A popular apologetic claim identifies this with the halocline effect at river-ocean estuaries, where density differences create a temporary boundary layer, framed as unknowable 7th-century knowledge. Critics raise two separate problems: first, the Arabic word used (bahr) means "sea" in classical usage, not "river" — the verse describes two seas meeting, not a river meeting an ocean, which doesn't match the estuary explanation apologists rely on; second, real estuary waters do eventually mix into brackish water rather than remaining permanently separated, which is at odds with the verse's language of a "forbidding" barrier that is never crossed.
