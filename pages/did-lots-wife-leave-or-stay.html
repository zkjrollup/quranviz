Contradictions

Did Lot's wife leave or never leave at all?

Surah 11:81 vs. 15:60, 26:171, 27:57, 27:135
“

Most verses describing Lot's wife say she was 'of those who remained behind' — she never left with the family at all, and was destroyed along with the rest of the disbelieving city. But 11:81 describes something structurally different: the angels tell Lot's family to travel by night and not look back, 'except your wife' — implying she was traveling with them, and would look back, which is why she was singled out.

Context This isn't a minor wording difference — it's two different explanations for how she died, and the ambiguity goes back further than modern criticism. Ibn Kathir's own tafsir on 11:81 acknowledges both readings as live options among early commentators: 'it is said that he did not take her along with him; it is also said that she did set out with them and turned round.' Quranic studies scholar Marijn van Putten has separately noted these two accounts are 'difficult to unify' as a single sequence of events.
