Scientific & Historical Absurdities

Are shooting stars missiles thrown at eavesdropping devils?

Surah 15:16-18, 37:6-10, 67:5
“

Stars are described as adorning the lowest heaven and serving as missiles hurled at devils (jinn) who try to eavesdrop on the angels' assembly, driving them away with a pursuing flame.

Context Some commentators read "shihāb" (translated "missile"/"flame") more broadly as a blaze or flare rather than a literal projectile, softening the mechanical claim. Read plainly, though, the text presents a real causal mechanism — meteors exist specifically to repel eavesdropping spirits — which doesn't correspond to the actual physics of meteoroids burning up on atmospheric entry. The concept closely parallels earlier Jewish and Zoroastrian mythology about stars guarding the heavens, suggesting shared pre-modern cosmology rather than a scientific claim.
