Historical Anachronisms

Is Mary, mother of Jesus, confused with Miriam, sister of Moses?

Surah 19:28, 66:12
“

Mary is addressed as "sister of Aaron" and identified as the daughter of "Imran" (Amram) — names that belong to Moses' sister Miriam and her father, who lived roughly 1,300 years before Mary.

Context Traditional tafsir explains "sister of Aaron" as an honorific for belonging to a priestly lineage (much as an Arabic speaker might call a pious man "brother of so-and-so" without a literal sibling relationship), not a genealogical claim. Critics find this explanation strained given how specifically Imran and Aaron are named elsewhere in the same text.
