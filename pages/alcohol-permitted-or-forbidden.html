Contradictions

Is alcohol permitted, cautioned against, or forbidden?

Surah 2:219, 4:43, 5:90, 16:67
“

Across four passages, alcohol goes from being listed as one of God's provisions, to a caution against praying while intoxicated, to "great sin, some benefit," to an outright abomination to avoid.

Context Widely read as a case of gradual revelation (tadrij) — moving a 7th-century Arabian society toward prohibition in stages rather than all at once. Critics read it as the text visibly changing its mind.
Contested Wording (Arabic & Transliteration)
Surah 16:67
تَتَّخِذُونَ مِنْهُ سَكَرًا وَرِزْقًا حَسَنًا
tattakhidhūna minhu sakaran wa rizqan ḥasanan
"you take from it intoxicant (sakar) and good provision"
Surah 4:43
لَا تَقْرَبُوا الصَّلَاةَ وَأَنتُمْ سُكَارَىٰ
lā taqrabū al-ṣalāta wa antum sukārā
"do not approach prayer while you are intoxicated"
Surah 2:219
فِيهِمَا إِثْمٌ كَبِيرٌ وَمَنَافِعُ لِلنَّاسِ
fīhimā ithmun kabīrun wa manāfi'u lil-nās
"in them is great sin, and [some] benefit for people"
Surah 5:90
رِجْسٌ مِّنْ عَمَلِ الشَّيْطَانِ فَاجْتَنِبُوهُ
rijsun min 'amali al-shayṭāni fa-jtanibūh
"an abomination from the handiwork of Satan, so avoid it"
