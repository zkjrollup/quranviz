Contradictions

Was Adam forgiven before or after being told to descend from paradise?

Surah 2:36-38 vs. 7:22-25 vs. 20:121-123
“

In Surah 20, Adam disobeys, is then chosen and forgiven by God, and only afterward is told to descend. In Surahs 2 and 7, the descent command is given immediately upon the transgression, with forgiveness described only afterward — reversing the order of the same two events.

Context Classical tafsir generally treats "descend" in these verses as a single decree announced at different narrative moments rather than a strict blow-by-blow timeline, and reads the descent as a change of abode/status rather than a punishment contingent on forgiveness being withheld. Critics argue the plain sequence of each account, taken on its own terms, puts these two events in a different order — forgiveness-then-descent in one telling, descent-then-forgiveness in the other — which is a genuine chronological tension, not just repeated narration.
Contested Wording (Arabic & Transliteration)
Surah 20:121-122
ثُمَّ اجْتَبَاهُ رَبُّهُ فَتَابَ عَلَيْهِ وَهَدَىٰ
thumma jtabāhu rabbuhu fa-tāba 'alayhi wa hadā
"then his Lord chose him, and turned to him in forgiveness, and guided him" — this happens before the descent command in verse 123
Surah 20:123
اهْبِطَا مِنْهَا جَمِيعًا
ihbiṭā minhā jamī'an
"get down from it, both of you" — the descent command, coming after forgiveness in this telling
Surah 2:36
فَأَزَلَّهُمَا الشَّيْطَانُ عَنْهَا فَأَخْرَجَهُمَا... وَقُلْنَا اهْبِطُوا
fa-azallahumā ash-shayṭānu 'anhā fa-akhrajahumā... wa qulnā ihbiṭū
"Satan caused them to slip... and We said, get down" — the descent command is given immediately upon the transgression, with no forgiveness mentioned yet
Surah 2:37
فَتَلَقَّىٰ آدَمُ مِن رَّبِّهِ كَلِمَاتٍ فَتَابَ عَلَيْهِ
fa-talaqqā ādamu min rabbihi kalimātin fa-tāba 'alayhi
"then Adam received words from his Lord, and He turned to him in forgiveness" — placed after the descent command in this telling, reversing Surah 20's order
