Cruelty & Violence

Does the Quran describe skin being regrown specifically so torment can continue?

Surah 4:56
“

Describes those who reject God's signs as being burned in Fire; whenever their skin is thoroughly burned, God will replace it with new skin so they may taste the punishment again — described as an expression of God's might and wisdom.

Context Traditional theology frames this as illustrating the totality and justice of divine punishment (Al-'Aziz, Al-Hakim) rather than gratuitous cruelty — sensation and accountability continuing without a merciful end for unrepentant rejection. This is a different genre from the earthly legal punishments elsewhere in this category (a description of the afterlife, not a command directed at believers to carry out), though critics note it's still a deliberately engineered mechanism for perpetuating physical suffering, presented without qualification or limit.
