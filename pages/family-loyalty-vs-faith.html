Intolerance

Does the Quran instruct believers not to ally with their own fathers and brothers?

Surah 9:23
“

Instructs believers not to take their fathers or brothers as allies if they prefer disbelief over faith, calling anyone who does so a wrongdoer.

Context Classical and modern commentary (Ma'arif al-Qur'an, and mainstream apologetics) situate this in a specific historical context: early Muslim converts whose own relatives fought against them at Badr and Uhud, where the verse addresses active battlefield allegiance, not ordinary family relations. Other verses (e.g. 31:15) explicitly instruct kindness toward disbelieving parents even while maintaining religious boundaries. Critics note the verse's plain wording is not limited to combat scenarios, and that it has real, documented modern relevance for people who face family estrangement over leaving or questioning the faith.
