Contradictions

Is intercession on the Day of Judgment possible, or not?

Surah 2:255, 20:109 vs. 2:48, 2:123, 74:48
“

Some verses allow intercession with God's permission; others state plainly that no intercession will be accepted on that day.

Context Classical theology reconciles these by reading the "no intercession" verses as excluding intercession *without* permission — the permission clause is doing a lot of work in that reconciliation.
Contested Wording (Arabic & Transliteration)
Surah 2:255
إِلَّا بِإِذْنِهِ
illā bi-idhnihi
"except by His permission" — the permission clause
Surah 20:109
إِلَّا مَنْ أَذِنَ لَهُ الرَّحْمَٰنُ
illā man adhina lahu ar-Raḥmān
"except one whom the Most Merciful permits"
Surah 2:48
وَلَا يُقْبَلُ مِنْهَا شَفَاعَةٌ
wa lā yuqbalu minhā shafā'ah
"nor will intercession (shafā'ah) be accepted from it"
Surah 2:123
وَلَا يُقْبَلُ مِنْهَا عَدْلٌ
wa lā yuqbalu minhā 'adl
nearly identical wording to 2:48
Surah 74:48
فَمَا تَنفَعُهُمْ شَفَاعَةُ الشَّافِعِينَ
fa-mā tanfa'uhum shafā'atu ash-shāfi'īn
"the intercession of intercessors will not benefit them"
