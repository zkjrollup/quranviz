Scientific & Historical Absurdities

Are there seven physical heavens held up without visible pillars?

Surah 2:29, 13:2, 41:12, 67:3, 71:15
“

The heavens are described as seven layers, raised "without pillars that you can see," each containing its own order, with the lowest adorned by stars.

Context A popular modern apologetic reading treats "without pillars you can see" as hinting at invisible physical forces (like gravity) holding celestial bodies in place. Historians of religion more commonly read "seven heavens" as a widely shared cosmological model across the ancient Near East (also found in Babylonian and Jewish sources) describing physical layered domes, not a claim compatible with modern astrophysics, which doesn't describe the universe as seven discrete layers at all.
