Contradictions

Did creation take six days or eight?

Surah 7:54 vs. 41:9–12
“

The heavens and earth are created in "six days" in several passages — but summing the stages described in Surah 41 (2 + 4 + 2 days) totals eight.

Context Classical exegetes have long debated whether these day-counts overlap or add — a genuinely contested reading, not a settled error.
Contested Wording (Arabic & Transliteration)
Surah 7:54, 10:3, 11:7
فِي سِتَّةِ أَيَّامٍ
fī sittati ayyāmin
"in six days" — the repeated phrase for the whole creation
Surah 41:9
خَلَقَ الْأَرْضَ فِي يَوْمَيْنِ
khalaqa al-arḍa fī yawmayn
"created the earth in two days"
Surah 41:10
فِي أَرْبَعَةِ أَيَّامٍ سَوَاءً لِّلسَّائِلِينَ
fī arba'ati ayyāmin sawā'an lil-sā'ilīn
"in four days, equal [i.e. complete] for those who ask" — whether this 4 includes or adds to the prior 2 is the crux of the arithmetic dispute
Surah 41:12
فِي يَوْمَيْنِ
fī yawmayn
"in two days" — for the seven heavens
